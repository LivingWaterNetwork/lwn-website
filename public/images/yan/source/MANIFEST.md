### National expansion notes

- **NASA image**: The `national-usa-night-lights-nasa.jpg` is NASA's well-known "City Lights of the United States, 2012" composite, assembled from Suomi NPP satellite VIIRS day-night-band data collected April–October 2012. Confirmed via the official NASA Earth Observatory article page (science.nasa.gov). As a work of the U.S. federal government, it is in the public domain — no license verification beyond confirming the genuine NASA source was needed. Downloaded the full-size `dnb_united_states_lrg.jpg` (6646×4430, ~7.8MB) directly from NASA's own asset host (`assets.science.nasa.gov`).
- **NYC images**: Both skyline photos were confirmed via their Unsplash pages to be geotagged/described as New York City (Manhattan aerial; Brooklyn Bridge + Manhattan skyline). The Times Square street photo is confirmed NYC via its Pexels description ("bustling nighttime scene in Times Square, NYC"). It is a touristic/central scene rather than a residential neighborhood street (no free, verified Brooklyn-brownstone-street photo was found in this pass) — caption accordingly if a more "neighborhood" feel is needed.
- **LA images**: Both skyline photos are confirmed downtown Los Angeles (one shot toward DTLA, one from Kenneth Hahn State Recreation Area) — intentionally avoiding the Hollywood-sign cliché per instructions. The Venice Beach boardwalk photo is confirmed Los Angeles, CA via its Pexels location metadata and serves as the street-level/community scene; no free, verified Silver Lake or Echo Park street-level photo was found in this pass.
- **Young adults photos**: Two new generic diverse-young-adults-gathering stock photos were sourced (distinct from the two already in the original Atlanta manifest) — one for NYC, one for LA. Both are intentionally generic (not depicting an actual YAN event) and should be captioned accordingly, consistent with the existing Atlanta young-adults photos.
- Two Unsplash+ premium candidates for a "diverse friends talking/hanging out" scene were found and rejected during this pass (photo IDs `LV44jbUvki0` and `FOkhwaU3in0`) — confirmed premium via their lock icons and "Unsplash+ License" text, so not used.
- Unsplash and Pexels photo pages return an anti-bot challenge to direct `curl` requests; pages were verified and direct CDN image URLs (images.unsplash.com / images.pexels.com) were resolved via an authenticated fetch tool, then downloaded directly from the CDN.
