- All photos were individually verified on their live photo pages before download to confirm they are under the **free** Unsplash License or Pexels License (not Unsplash+/premium). - The Oakland Cemetery church photo is geographically confirmed as Atlanta, GA (Oakland Cemetery, Oakland Avenue SE) per the photo's own location metadata — a historic Atlanta structure suitable for the "architecture/historic" theme.

- Original photo pages should be re-checked periodically in case a photographer changes distribution terms in the future (rare, but license pages note terms can be updated going forward for new users, not retroactively).
