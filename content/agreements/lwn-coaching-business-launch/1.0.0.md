# Living Water Network

## 60-Day Coaching & Business Launch Agreement

**Prepared for {{participant_name}} \| {{participant_business}}**

**Journey dates:** {{journey_start_display}} – {{journey_end_display}}

This Agreement is between **{{org_legal_name}} ("LWN")**, an Atlanta-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit located at **{{org_street_address}}, {{org_city_state}} {{org_postal_code}}**, and **{{participant_name}} ("Participant")**, signing personally.

**{{coach_name}}** will serve as Participant's coach and primary contact. **{{partner_name}} ("{{partner_short}}")** will deliver the branding and website services described below under a separate arrangement with LWN. LWN remains responsible to Participant for coordinating and delivering the commitments in this Agreement.

## 1. Purpose of the Journey

LWN will walk alongside Participant through a personalized, Christ-centered coaching journey that supports spiritual formation, faithful stewardship, financial stability, and the full launch of {{participant_business}}.

The journey combines personal coaching, practical financial education, business planning, branding, and website development. Participant's spiritual growth will not be measured by business performance.

Financial stability and business launch are goals toward which the parties will work. Specific income, savings, client acquisition, health, or spiritual outcomes are not guaranteed.

## 2. Duration and Coaching Sessions

The coaching journey begins **{{journey_start_weekday_display}}**, and concludes **{{journey_end_weekday_display}}**, unless extended by written agreement or under the health-emergency provision.

The journey includes **one {{session_length_minutes}}-minute Zoom coaching call each week**, with {{session_count}} calls anticipated across the program period.

Participant will review her schedule and provide a generally suitable recurring day. The parties will arrange individual call times by text, allowing reasonable flexibility around their schedules. Any holiday adjustments will be coordinated in advance.

Text messages and email may be used to confirm scheduling and routine approvals.

## 3. LWN Coaching and Formation

{{coach_name}} will personally coach Participant using LWN's *At the Table* curriculum as the starting foundation.

The journey follows **See → Sit → Send**:

- **See:** Understand Participant's current season, priorities, responsibilities, resources, and growth goals.
- **Sit:** Engage Scripture, prayer, reflection, coaching, and practical exercises.
- **Send:** Apply learning to daily life, relationships, financial decisions, and business responsibilities.

Coaching will address LWN's six spheres of formation: **Spiritual, Mental, Emotional, Physical, Relational, and Stewardship**. Emphasis and pacing will reflect Participant's needs rather than requiring equal attention to every sphere.

Included materials and support consist of:

- A personalized journey plan and agreed priorities.
- Guided reflection and practical action steps.
- Regular accountability and progress review.
- A closing review and next-season action plan.

Physical stewardship may include discussion of rest, routines, energy, and sustainable habits. **In-person physical training is optional, costs extra, and requires advance agreement on the provider, scope, schedule, and price.**

## 4. Financial Stewardship and Business Planning

LWN will provide financial education and practical planning support, including:

- Review of the financial information Participant supplies.
- A 60-day spending and savings plan.
- Debt inventory and repayment planning.
- Personal and business budget development.
- Identification of business startup and recurring expenses.
- Accountability and adjustments during the journey.

Participant remains responsible for the accuracy of her information and for all financial decisions, accounts, payments, filings, and business obligations.

This work provides coaching and education. It does not include investment management, tax preparation, legal representation, or licensed clinical services.

## 5. Business Establishment Framework

Participant will receive a practical checklist to help establish and launch {{participant_business}}. It will identify tasks, responsible parties, priorities, and completion status.

The framework will address, as applicable:

- Business structure, registrations, and professional requirements.
- Banking, bookkeeping, budgeting, and expense tracking.
- Service selection, pricing, capacity, and operating costs.
- Insurance and professional support needs.
- Booking, payment collection, cancellation policies, and client communications.
- Brand assets, website readiness, and launch activities.
- Client permissions, testimonials, and marketing materials.

LWN and {{partner_short}} will help Participant identify and organize these requirements. Government filings, professional approvals, insurance purchases, and third-party services remain Participant's responsibility unless separately agreed in writing.

## 6. {{participant_business}} Branding

{{partner_short}} will refine a selected visual direction using Participant's shared materials, approved references, and the brand concepts already developed.

The final digital branding package will include:

- Primary logo.
- Secondary logo or alternate layout.
- Brand mark.
- Color palette.
- Typography recommendations.
- A concise brand reference guide.
- Final logo files in suitable digital and vector formats.

Participant will approve the direction before final production.

**Social media templates, printed materials, product packaging, product formulation, and publication of books or journals are outside this Agreement.**

## 7. Website Development

{{partner_short}} will deliver a complete, mobile-responsive {{participant_business}} website with up to {{website_max_pages}} pages:

1. Home.
2. About.
3. Services.
4. Frequently Asked Questions.
5. Contact / Book.

The website scope includes:

- Design aligned with the approved brand.
- Copy development and refinement using Participant's supplied information.
- Integration with an existing booking platform through supported links or standard embeds.
- A standard contact form.
- Basic on-page SEO, including page titles, descriptions, and heading structure.
- Domain connection, launch testing, and a basic handoff walkthrough.

The website has an **estimated customary project value of approximately {{website_customary_value_display}}**. This is a description of the offered website service, not a tax valuation or an additional charge.

Custom booking software, an online store, memberships, courses, product development, and ongoing marketing campaigns require a separate written scope.

Participant must approve service descriptions, credentials, prices, policies, and public-facing claims before publication. Draft ideas in her source materials will not automatically be treated as current offerings.

The parties will work toward launch during the journey. The launch schedule depends on timely materials, approvals, platform access, and business readiness. Any required adjustment will be communicated and documented.

## 8. Revisions and Post-Launch Edits

The branding and website work includes **{{revision_rounds}} total revision rounds across the combined project**.

A revision round is one consolidated set of feedback on work presented for review. Revisions refine the agreed direction and scope. A new direction after approval, additional pages, or new functionality requires a written scope adjustment.

Corrections needed to make the work match the approved scope do not count as revision rounds.

{{partner_short}} will provide **{{post_launch_days}} calendar days of post-launch support and edits**, beginning on the official launch date confirmed in writing. Included edits cover reasonable updates to existing text, images, links, and layout, together with correction of website defects within the delivered scope.

New pages, redesigns, and additional functionality are outside post-launch edit coverage. Requests submitted within the support window will be scheduled for completion even if the work finishes after that window.

An optional ongoing retainer may be discussed at a significantly discounted rate. It requires a separate agreement and does not renew automatically.

## 9. Participant Expenses

Participant is responsible for domain registration and renewal, hosting, booking software, payment processing, subscriptions, premium assets, and other approved third-party expenses.

These expenses are additional to the {{total_commitment_display}} commitment and will be incorporated into her business budget.

LWN and {{partner_short}} will obtain Participant's approval before incurring expenses on her behalf. Wherever practical, business accounts will be established in Participant's name and under her control.

## 10. Payment and Mission Allocation

Participant agrees to a total financial commitment of **{{total_commitment_display}}**, paid directly to LWN through the payment link LWN provides.

The parties intend the following allocation:

- **{{mission_allocation_display}} toward LWN's mission**, which Participant intends as a charitable contribution.
- **{{partner_allocation_display}} toward {{partner_short}}'s branding and website services**, paid through LWN's arrangement with {{partner_short}}.

LWN and {{partner_short}} remain separate organizations and will maintain appropriate records of their respective transactions.

LWN (a 501(c)(3) organization, EIN {{org_ein}}) will provide appropriate payment documentation and any required charitable acknowledgment or disclosure. Participant should consult her tax adviser regarding eligibility and treatment.

If a payment is missed, LWN will contact Participant before suspending work. Any revised payment dates must be agreed in writing.

## 11. Communication and Participation

Participant may contact {{coach_name}} through text, email, or voice notes during regular business hours. Additional check-ins may be arranged as needed.

The parties will establish business hours and communication expectations at onboarding. Support is provided as reasonably available; immediate responses and unscheduled calls are not guaranteed.

Participant agrees to participate honestly, complete mutually agreed action steps, and provide timely materials and feedback. When a step becomes unrealistic, the parties will discuss an adjustment.

## 12. Rescheduling, Cancellation, and Health Emergencies

Either party should provide at least **{{reschedule_notice_hours}} hours' notice** to reschedule a call. A Participant cancellation with less notice or a missed appointment may count as that week's session, except in a qualifying emergency. Sessions canceled by the coach will be rescheduled.

Except as provided below or required by law, Participant's commitment is final. A change of mind, ordinary scheduling conflict, or decision not to participate does not automatically cancel the remaining payment obligation or create a refund entitlement.

A **qualifying health emergency** is a serious illness, injury, or medical event that substantially prevents Participant from working or participating in the journey.

Participant or her representative should notify LWN as reasonably possible. LWN will work in good faith to document an appropriate pause, extension, or revised schedule, including any payment-date adjustment. Detailed medical records are not required; reasonable confirmation may be requested.

If the emergency makes continuation impracticable, the parties will review termination, amounts already paid, completed work, and outstanding commitments in good faith. No specific refund is promised by this provision.

If LWN cannot deliver a material commitment and cannot resolve the issue within a reasonable period after written notice, Participant may end the affected services and receive a refund of prepaid amounts attributable to undelivered services.

## 13. Privacy and Confidentiality

Access to Participant's materials is limited to authorized personnel working within or on behalf of LWN or {{partner_short}} who need access to perform their assigned work and are subject to confidentiality obligations.

Private coaching reflections and financial records will be restricted to personnel directly supporting that work. {{partner_short}}'s branding and website personnel will receive relevant business information and only those personal themes Participant specifically approves for that purpose.

Participant's testimony, photographs, financial results, coaching reflections, and business materials will not be used publicly, in a case study, or in a portfolio without separate permission.

Calls will not be recorded without Participant's agreement. Confidentiality may be limited where disclosure is legally required or necessary to address an immediate threat of serious harm.

## 14. Ownership and Handoff

Participant retains ownership of all materials she supplies.

Upon full payment, Participant will own the final custom branding, website design, copy, and project-specific source files created for {{participant_business}} under this Agreement. {{partner_short}} will transfer its rights in those final deliverables and provide the available files, access, and handoff materials.

LWN retains ownership of its pre-existing curriculum, coaching methods, and reusable resources. Participant may use the supplied coaching materials for her personal development.

Third-party software, fonts, stock assets, and tools remain subject to their respective licenses. Any material licensing or transfer limitations will be disclosed before use.

## 15. Changes and Agreement

Changes to price, scope, ownership, or duration require written agreement. Text or email approval is sufficient when the proposed change is clearly described and accepted.

This Agreement contains the parties' understanding concerning this journey. It may be signed electronically and becomes effective when both parties sign.
