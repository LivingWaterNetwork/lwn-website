projectType: Initiative Launch & Digital Platform
summary: A digital home for a young-adult ministry network that began in Atlanta and is opening hubs in additional cities — church directory, events, leaders, prayer, stories, resources, and its own administrative backend.
challenge: >-
  Young-adult ministries in a city often work in parallel without knowing about each other.
  YAN needed a place where those ministries could be found, where events across churches
  could be seen together, and where the same structure could be repeated in a new city
  without rebuilding it each time.
approach: >-
  We built YAN as its own branded section with a distinct visual identity, isolated from the
  parent organization's design system so neither bleeds into the other. The information
  architecture is city-scoped from the ground up — a single city registry drives the city
  selector, navigation, and cross-city callouts, so opening a new hub is a content change
  rather than a rebuild. An administrative interface lets the team manage groups, leaders,
  events, resources, stories, and prayer content without touching code.
workCompleted:
  - Separate YAN brand system — namespaced color palette, typography, and component layer
  - City-scoped information architecture driven by a single city registry
  - City hub pages for Atlanta plus additional launching hubs
  - Church and ministry network directory
  - Events system with per-city listings, detail pages, calendar export, and registration
  - Leaders, prayer, stories, and resources sections, each city-scoped
  - Public submission forms for joining, nominating leaders, suggesting groups, and submitting stories and resources
  - Authenticated admin interface for managing every content type
  - Newsletter subscription and notification routing to the initiative's own inbox
  - Dedicated navigation and footer that replace the parent-site chrome within the section
currentStage: Atlanta is the founding hub and is live. Additional city hubs are published in a launch posture while local teams are formed.
status: in-development

outcomes:
  - A single registry-driven structure that scales to new cities without a rebuild
  - Team-managed content across every section through the admin interface
  - Public participation paths for individuals, leaders, and ministries

YAN is the clearest example of what "initiative launch" means in practice: brand, information
architecture, public site, participation funnels, and an administrative backend, built so the
next city costs a fraction of the first.
