challenge: >-
  Hand of Life Renovations serves two audiences that look for very different things. A
  homeowner planning a kitchen or bathroom remodel wants to see finished work and know the
  company is licensed and insured. A property manager handling unit turns and common-area
  upgrades is evaluating capacity and reliability. One generic contractor site would have
  served neither well, and the business needed inquiries to arrive without a phone call
  being the only way in.
approach: >-
  We split the site along those two audiences, giving residential and multifamily their own
  sections with their own language, while keeping one consistent identity across the whole
  site. A gallery of completed work carries the proof that a contractor's reputation depends
  on, and a quote request is reachable from anywhere on the site rather than living on a
  single contact page.

  - Quote request flow, with phone and email as direct alternatives
