## PART 1: COPYRIGHT REGISTRATION (U.S. Copyright Office)

Your work is **automatically copyrighted the moment it is created** under U.S. law. However, registering with the Copyright Office is strongly recommended — it creates a public record and is required before you can sue for infringement or claim statutory damages.

### What to Register
Register the complete work: **"At the Table: A Formation Guide for Kingdom Leaders"** by Omar J. Fandino, published by Living Water Network Inc.

### How to Register

**Step 1 — Go to the registration portal**
[copyright.gov/registration](https://www.copyright.gov/registration/) → Select **"Literary Works"**

**Step 2 — Choose your filing type**
- **eCO (Electronic Copyright Office)** — recommended; fastest processing; lower fee
- Paper filing is available but slower and costs more

**Step 3 — Complete the application**
You will need:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Type of Work | Literary Work |
| Title | At the Table: A Formation Guide for Kingdom Leaders |
| Year of Completion | 2026 |
| Published? | Yes (if you've distributed it) or No (if still in draft) |
| Publication Date | The date you first distributed it publicly (even to a cohort) |
| Author | Omar J. Fandino |
| Author's Contribution | Text (entire work) |
| Claimant / Rights Holder | Living Water Network Inc. |
| Nation of First Publication | United States |

> **Note on Author vs. Claimant:** If you wrote it as an individual but want LWN to hold rights, you can assign copyright to LWN as the claimant. Consult a copyright attorney if you want to structure this precisely — this affects who can license the work.

**Step 4 — Upload the deposit copy**
The Copyright Office requires a copy of the work. For an unpublished work: upload a PDF. For a published work: two complete copies are required (can be digital upload for eCO).

**Step 5 — Pay the fee**
- Single-author, single work filed electronically: **$65** (as of 2025 — confirm current fee at copyright.gov)
- Paper filing: $125

**Step 6 — Processing time**
- Electronic: 3–9 months for a certificate (you are protected from the registration date, not the certificate date)
- Expedited processing ("special handling") is available for $800 — only needed if litigation is imminent

### What You Receive
A Certificate of Registration with an official registration number. Keep this on file. Place the copyright notice in your book:

```
© 2026 Living Water Network Inc. All rights reserved.
No portion of this guide may be reproduced without written permission
from Living Water Network Inc.
www.lwnetwork.org
```

## PART 2: ISBN (International Standard Book Number)

An ISBN is not legally required, but it is **essential for distribution** — bookstores, Amazon, libraries, and distributors all require one. Each format (print, PDF, ebook) gets its own ISBN.

### The Official U.S. ISBN Agency
**Bowker** is the sole official ISBN agency in the United States.
Website: [myidentifiers.com](https://www.myidentifiers.com) (Bowker's consumer portal)

### Cost
| Package | ISBNs | Price (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Single ISBN | 1 | ~$125 |
| 10 ISBNs | 10 | ~$295 |
| 100 ISBNs | 100 | ~$575 |

> **Recommendation:** Purchase the **10-pack**. You will want separate ISBNs for the print edition, the digital/PDF edition, and future revised editions. If you publish a Facilitator Guide as a separate volume, that needs its own ISBN too.

### How to Register

1. Create an account at [myidentifiers.com](https://www.myidentifiers.com)
2. Purchase your ISBN package
3. Register your title — you will complete:
   - **Title:** At the Table: A Formation Guide for Kingdom Leaders
   - **Author:** Omar J. Fandino
   - **Publisher:** Living Water Network Inc.
   - **Publisher address:** (your Georgia address or PO Box)
   - **Format:** Assign separate ISBNs per format
   - **Publication date:** Anticipated or confirmed date
   - **Price:** Set your list price (even if you distribute free — list a price for catalog purposes; you can note "free to invited participants")
   - **BISAC Category codes:** Choose subject categories — recommended:
     - `REL012130` — Religion / Christian Living / Spiritual Growth
     - `REL067000` — Religion / Leadership
     - `REL012000` — Religion / Christian Living / General

4. Bowker will issue your ISBN(s) immediately after purchase.

### Barcode
Once you have an ISBN, you need a **barcode** for the back cover (required for retail/library distribution). Bowker sells barcodes (~$25 each), or you can generate one free from [ISBN.org](https://www.isbn.org) or third-party tools. The barcode encodes your ISBN-13 plus price.

Place the barcode in the **lower right corner of the back cover** — standard publishing convention.

## PART 3: LIBRARY OF CONGRESS CONTROL NUMBER (LCCN)

Optional but adds institutional credibility — used by libraries to catalog your book.

**Apply before publication** at: [loc.gov/publish/pcn](https://www.loc.gov/publish/pcn/)

- Free of charge
- Takes a few weeks
- You list the LCCN on your copyright page: `Library of Congress Control Number: XXXXXXXXX`
- After publishing, mail two copies of the book to the Copyright Office (mandatory legal deposit for published works)

## PART 6: NOTES ON NONPROFIT PUBLISHING

Because LWN is a 501(c)(3), a few additional considerations:

- **Who holds copyright matters for grants and partnerships.** If donors or foundations fund the curriculum, confirm in your grant agreements that LWN retains copyright and can distribute freely.
- **"Free distribution" does not forfeit copyright.** You can distribute the guide free to cohort participants and still hold and enforce all copyright protections.
- **If you ever partner with a publisher**, you will negotiate these rights. Having your copyright registered and your ISBN in Bowker's database strengthens your negotiating position.
