01 — The Brand Story

Living water, for a connected world.

The mark holds three ideas at once. A globe — the world we are sent into. Living water that cradles and lifts it — the source of all true formation. And a network of nodes tracing the surface — people, joined together and rippling outward.

“Whoever drinks the water I give them will never thirst. The water I give will become in them a spring of water welling up to eternal life.”
John 4:14

Source

Formation begins at the spring, not the self. The water is the giver, not the work.

Spread

One drop becomes a ripple. The ripples in the mark are the movement reaching outward.

Sent

The world sits in the water, held and lifted — leaders formed to be poured out.

04 — Clear Space & Sizing

Give the mark room to breathe

Maintain clear space on all sides equal to the height of the globe icon. Keep this zone free of text, graphics, and edges.

07 — Typography

Typography

A three-part system: Poppins for the brand and headlines, Lora for scripture and reflective moments, and Lato for clean, readable body text. All three are free, open-source Google Fonts.

08 — Voice & Messaging

How we sound

Personal, pastoral, and present. We speak the way a trusted mentor speaks across a table — warm and direct, never corporate, never performative.

Personal

We write to a person, not an audience. “You,” not “users.”

✓ “You don't have to carry this alone.”
✕ “Stakeholders will be supported.”

Pastoral

Grounded in care and Scripture, gentle with people, honest about the hard parts.

✓ “Formation is slow, and that's grace.”
✕ “Maximize your spiritual ROI.”

Present

Active and now. We meet people where they are today.

✓ “Start where you are.”
✕ “In due course, one might begin.”

Invitational

We invite rather than instruct. The table has an open seat.

✓ “Pull up a chair.”
✕ “Applicants must comply.”
