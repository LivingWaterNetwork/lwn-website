# Measure & Make - Concept 03.5: The Maker's Signature

Concept 03.5 combines Concept 03's premium Maker's Seal system with the recognition discipline of the name-first direction.

## Primary rule

The complete **Measure & Make** name always leads. The Maker's Seal is a secondary signature used only after the name has been established.

Do not use `M&M` as a public abbreviation, monogram, favicon, social handle, or navigation label.

## Brand idea

- **Measure** represents discernment, clarity, evidence, architecture, and wise stewardship.
- **Make** represents execution, craftsmanship, systems, products, and durable outcomes.
- The **brass center** is accountable action: the decision point where measured understanding becomes form.
- The opposing forms represent judgment above and craft beneath.

This is an independent commercial identity. It should not inherit the LWN palette or combine with the LWN logo. While temporarily hosted on the LWN domain, use factual disclosure language that distinguishes the for-profit venture from LWN's charitable programs and donations.
